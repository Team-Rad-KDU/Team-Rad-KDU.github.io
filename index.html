Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What is Team Rad KDU?

Team Rad KDU is the student team of the Society of Radiography and Radiotherapy at the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences (FAHS), General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU), Sri Lanka. “Rad” stands for radiography. Each year the team runs SIHINA, a community-service project for World Radiography Day.

What is SIHINA (Sihina KDU)?

SIHINA (සිහින, “dreams” in Sinhala) is the Annual Radiography Day Celebration Project of the Society of Radiography and Radiotherapy, KDU. It has run since at least 2014, renovating schools, libraries and community facilities across Sri Lanka.

What is SIHINA 2026?

SIHINA 2026/27, led by Intake 40, is renovating an old classroom complex at Urugala Sri Dharmaloka School, Ingiriya, Horana, for about 400 students, with a proposed budget of Rs. 710,002. Full details, the budget and how to donate are on the SIHINA 2026 page: the SIHINA 2026 page

How can I support Team Rad KDU?

Sponsor or donate to SIHINA 2026 through the Google Form at sponsorship form, or see the bank details on the SIHINA 2026 page. You can also call the team on 078 713 4441.
